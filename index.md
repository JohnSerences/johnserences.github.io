Welcome to the Perception and Cognition Lab at the University of California, San Diego.

Our research focuses on understanding how behavioral goals influence perception, decision making, and memory. To investigate these questions, we combine psychophysics, computational modeling, and neuroimaging methods (fMRI and EEG) in human participants.

<div class="divider"></div>

We are based in the [Department of Psychology](https://psychology.ucsd.edu/) and participate in the [Neuroscience Graduate Program](https://neurograd.ucsd.edu/).
