John T. Serences, PhD

Professor, Department of Psychology
University of California, San Diego

John is a UCSD alum and worked with Prof. Hal Pashler as an undergraduate. He moved to Johns Hopkins for graduate school, where he trained with Prof. Steve Yantis, and then completed postdoctoral training at the Salk Institute with Dr. Geoff Boynton.

His lab investigates how behavioral goals shape perception, decision-making, and memory using psychophysics, computational modeling, and neuroimaging methods.
