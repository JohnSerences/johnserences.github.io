## Interested in joining the lab?

We welcome students and trainees who are excited about perception, attention, working memory, and computational cognitive neuroscience.

## Volunteer and Psych 199 opportunities

If you are interested in volunteering in the lab or enrolling as a Psych 199 student for course credit, please complete our RA application:

- [RA Application Form](https://goo.gl/forms/vgWux8c3P7c07jzi1)

You can also contact the lab coordinator at `pcclabcoordinator@ucsd.edu` and we will follow up when openings are available.

## Graduate students and postdocs

Prospective graduate students should apply through UC San Diego programs such as:

- [Psychology PhD Program](https://psychology.ucsd.edu/graduate-program/index.html)
- [Neurosciences Graduate Program](https://neurograd.ucsd.edu/)
